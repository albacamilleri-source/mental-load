Mental Load

Mental Load is a personal productivity application designed to help organise household and family administration.

Mental Load helps turn information into actionable tasks, organise personal planning, create and manage calendar events, and prepare email drafts.

Google integration

Mental Load uses Google Calendar access to create, manage and display calendar events requested by the user.

Mental Load uses Gmail access to create email drafts requested by the user.

Google account information is used only to provide these features.
